# Plan 06-02 - Feedback Classification And Pending Queue

## Objective

Extend `services/feedback-service/` into a service-local normalization, classification, dedupe, and pending-review queue library. This plan keeps feedback queues derived and human-reviewed; it must not alter the WeCom feedback channel or invoke legal skill modification.

## Threat Model

<threat_model>
<risk id="T-06-02-classification" severity="medium">Feedback may be misclassified or collapse distinct review needs. Mitigation: rule-based category mapping with explicit tests for all PRD categories.</risk>
<risk id="T-06-02-dedupe" severity="medium">Repeated callbacks or shim writes may create duplicate pending work. Mitigation: stable idempotency key handling and duplicate metadata preservation.</risk>
<risk id="T-06-02-human-review" severity="high">Route-mismatch feedback could trigger automatic legal skill changes. Mitigation: no imports or calls to skill optimization runners, queue metadata includes no_auto_modify, and tests assert requires_human_review.</risk>
<risk id="T-06-02-privacy" severity="high">Feedback callbacks may expose raw callback bodies or secrets. Mitigation: reuse exact raw payload rejection and expose only raw_payload_ref/source references.</risk>
</threat_model>
