## Critical Gap Closure

The prior audit reported two critical issues:

- `WeComSessionRuntime.handle_turn()` returned continue/start outcomes without submitting `runtime.create_run()`.
- No concrete service-level caller invoked `WeComSessionRuntime.handle_turn()` from sanitized WeCom ingress.

Phase 19 closed both:

- `services/wecom-service/src/wecom_service/session_runtime.py` now calls `runtime.create_run()` for `CONTINUE_SESSION` and `START_NEW_SESSION`, returns the created run id, preserves `resume_interrupt()` for pending clarifications, and keeps active-task follow-ups on the fast control plane.
- `services/wecom-service/src/wecom_service/ingress_runtime.py` adds `WeComRuntimeIngress`, a pure host-callable callback-to-runtime boundary that normalizes sanitized callbacks, builds `SessionScope` and `RunRequest`, and invokes `WeComSessionRuntime.handle_turn()`.
- `services/wecom-service/tests/test_session_interrupt_runtime.py` and `services/wecom-service/tests/test_ingress_runtime.py` cover continue, start-new, duplicate create-run idempotency, clarification resume, active-task no-run behavior, callback ingress wiring, and bounded context metadata refs.
