| T-78.1-07 | Denial of Service | `vision_resolver` without store | mitigate | Fail-closed on image-bearing turns if no store/vision runner |
| T-78.1-08 | Elevation of Privilege | WeCom channel enabled by single knob | mitigate | `load_wecom_channel_config` preserves dual-knob AND |
| T-78.1-SC | Tampering | package installs | accept | Internal package dependencies only |
