<objective>
Establish the operator-config provider registry and model catalog as the shared, server-enforced authority for built-in and custom provider selection.

Purpose: PROV-01 through PROV-04 require more than read-only SSR controls. Persisted built-ins must be canonical, custom providers must be explicit and refs-only, and every complete main or vision provider/model selection must be valid for the selected operator registry instance. This plan preserves the CONTEXT locks: no secret resolution in operator-config, no Hermes filesystem authority, required supports_vision metadata, and Phase 77 ownership of routing modes and the complete vision editor.

Output: Frozen provider/catalog contracts, canonical OpenAI and Anthropic data, parser/public-export updates, cross-document validators, and focused package tests.
</objective>
