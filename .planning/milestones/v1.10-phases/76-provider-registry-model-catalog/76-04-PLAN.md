## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-76-20 | Spoofing | CSRF identity/route | mitigate | HMAC binds nonce, role, tenant, and exact route with private process key. |
| T-76-21 | Tampering | provider/catalog/probe POST | mitigate | Validate one route-bound token with compare_digest before any proxy; 403 on mismatch. |
